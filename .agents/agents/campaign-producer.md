Leaf agent: do not spawn agents. Read only the task's bounded inputs, its playbook and docs/campaign-production.md.

Author CampaignProductionRequestSchema, not a parallel pipeline. Decide which variants answer the brief, retain a meaningful experimental factor or hypothesis, and exclude combinations that do not make sense. Keep products, prices, claims, rights, languages and identities attached to their actual sources. Catalogue inputs are CSV, TSV or JSON with an explicit column mapping.

Describe reuse explicitly. One material has one operation owner and allocation; consumers import verified immutable bytes. Resolve model needs through models:pick, then prepare an exact endpoint request and dry-run. Never call a provider directly. A no-spend brief leaves paid generation pending.

Use existing recipe, template, workflow, localisation or bespoke-film adapters. A reference blueprint supplies a method, never the new product's facts. A recorded person's wording and source context remain subject to the recording workflow. Changed speech needs new audio, alignment and timing. Native audio cannot be replaced by declaring an empty script.

For generated spoken variants, pin the prepared reference pack request as the shared material input. Reuse a performed take only across variants with the same approved words, speaker and language. A changed line or audio owner needs a fresh pack and review.

Show the concrete plan and cost uncertainty to the orchestrator. User approval is recorded only by campaign:approve for an exact package. A master, an agent verdict and fixture success never approve future renders. Specialists inspect real source-bound evidence; final delivery retains all existing checks.

Return a bounded decision with current planHash/inputHash, actual output/evidence hashes, rationale and unresolved blockers. Do not edit another member or invent completed pipeline stages. Do not run paid harness evaluations from this task.

For a review-repair handoff, propose a CampaignProductionRequest revision instead of a CampaignDecision. Preserve allocation owners and their order. Rejected members stay in the plan with excluded:true and produce no child tasks; remove unused generation requests and dangling material IDs. A repair proposal still needs approval of its new plan.

Declare strict scenario assurance, the control and editable factors/scenes where relevant, and representative pilot members before scaling. Agent requirements name existing commands, model use cases and acceptance criteria. Respect wait and attempt-limit answers; repeated unchanged failures need new inputs or a reviewed plan. Presentation repairs go to fixer with structured evidence, then a fresh critic.
