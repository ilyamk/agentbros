# Recipe direction

Social: make a concrete promise, establish just enough context, demonstrate, pay it off. A loop can return to the opening composition, but should not conceal a missing resolution.

Product: show starting state → real action → result. A detail shot should prove a material/function claim; a screen recording should keep the control and result readable. Avoid a montage of generic screenshots presented as a demonstration.

Education: question → prerequisite definition → worked example → application → recap. Explain the causal step before showing the exception. State what is simplified and cite factual claims. A chart hold may span multiple spoken beats when the argument is unchanged.

Documentary: establish who/where/when, introduce verified evidence, distinguish interpretation, resolve the central question. Source dates and uncertainty matter. A dramatic crop must not change the meaning of the original evidence.

Music motion: plan the beat accents, energy arc and one repeating visual motif. Change one parameter at a time for variation. Tempo metadata is an authored plan until measured; template selection is not automatic music analysis.

Good example 1: a 30s product demo uses one click at real speed, holds the resulting state, then states the benefit. Good example 2: a 3min lesson holds the same diagram while explaining a mechanism, then works an independent example and recaps. Failure: every beat swaps style, mood and camera direction with no change in the argument.

Recording direction: choose shot size from the evidence needed. A face supplies delivery; a hand/detail supplies an action; a wide establishes geography. Maintain screen direction and light. Ask a presenter for an intention (“help the viewer see the cause”), then define the pickup line/gesture, rather than requesting generic “more energy”.

Review rubric: promised outcome delivered, one purpose per beat, proof actually supports claim, speech fits allocated time, continuity holds, captions/graphics do not compete, music arc supports delivery, output format preserves focal point. Record a specific fix and compare the next preview at the same moment.

## Concrete product advertising workflow

When permitted product originals, real interaction footage, source-backed proof, recorded speech/word timings and music already exist, route to [the product advertising workflow](../../../../docs/workflows/product-ad.md) instead of retaining an animatic placeholder. Write five concrete beats and attach each claim to exact supplied evidence; plan checks byte hashes, crop coverage, speech/caption correspondence and media ranges without generation calls. Create only a new project, render with `--timeline`, inspect all three clean/captioned variants and pass through strict final delivery. Pending human review is not success or a creative approval. The reproducible synthetic local example is a technical fixture, never a source of production speech, rights or product metrics.
