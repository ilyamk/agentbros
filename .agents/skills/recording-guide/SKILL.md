# Recording guide

Read the approved brief, relevant project artefacts and `AGENTS.md` first. Use this skill when its decision is needed; do not invoke every craft skill for a simple edit. Keep leaf-agent work at depth one. Existing approval and no-spend instructions take precedence.

## Decision and output

Produce `capture-plan` in `pipeline/craft/recording-guide.json` (or the owning stage artefact when its schema already covers these fields): shotlist, room tone, dialogue version, take IDs, exposure/white balance lock, clean background, focus, mic check, slate, coverage and source consent. Distinguish observed facts, artistic choices and unresolved assumptions. Preserve approved source/version pins.

Express the decision in the owning brief/script/design/storyboard artefact before generating assets. Tools and generation permissions come from the creative-studio router.

## Worked decisions

- Record one continuous presenter take and ten seconds of room tone before B-roll.
- Film the same product action wide and close with consistent screen direction and light.

Failure to avoid: Discover clipped dialogue only after dismantling the recording setup.

## Alternatives and review

A phone and quiet room can suffice: monitor a short test, preserve original files, and avoid automatic beauty processing for identity-critical shots.

Review: Does every decision serve the brief? Is there concrete evidence for technical/factual claims? Are timing, continuity, rights and cost assumptions explicit? Can another agent reproduce or revise the decision from the recorded artefact? Use [evaluation briefs](../creative-studio/references/evaluation.md) for review; heuristic scores do not prove audience response.
