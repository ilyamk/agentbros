Read [caption decisions](references/caption-decisions.md) for grouping and translation judgment, and [caption styles](references/caption-styles.md) to choose a burned style for a user's footage (ten presets with outline, shadow or box backing), the readability limits and safe zones, and how a timeline selects it (`caption-style` edit command). Registered profiles: karaoke, phrase hold/fade and keyword emphasis. Inputs are ordered source word timestamps; `src/types/templates.ts` validates the local seconds-based word contract. Preserve source words and timing when editing emphasis. Do not guess forced alignment or claim a transcription service was run without its artifact.

Subtitles on a user's own recording start from a `TranscriptSchema` file (`speech:local` free, `transcribe` paid on approval); the order is in [footage editing](../../../docs/footage-editing.md). Ask which style when the user has not said.

`transcribe` also supports `--provider fal --endpoint <id>` within the budgeted recording route; see [speech endpoints](../../../docs/fal-catalogue.md#verified-endpoints). Preserve estimated timing flags; fal output does not establish verbatim filler coverage.

Choose profile from reading task: karaoke for speech tracking, phrase for calm education/documentary, keyword for one deliberate term. `scene.template` accepts caption profiles as overlays. Talking-head source alignment continues through its dedicated pipeline; do not duplicate presenter audio to obtain captions. Confirm timebase and source offsets when reusing footage.

For an existing local ASR index explicitly carrying native word evidence, follow the [estimated word proposal route](../../../docs/offline-word-timing.md). `pnpm media:words --project ID --index INDEX.json --output public/projects/ID/pipeline/words-proposal.json` exports original-source intervals for review. Keep its source/evidence hashes and `estimated`/`reviewRequired` status. Listen to exact starts, ends and pauses before authoring captions; ASR text may omit or change words. This proposal does not replace forced alignment or approve captions. Do not invent confidence/loss, stretch collapsed words, or derive target frames from nominal VFR FPS. Preserve the proposal separately from any corrected transcript and use the owning workflow's checked source mapping.

For diagnostics on the six audited existing EN/RU AI/TTS inputs, read the [fixed corpus route](../recording-recut/references/analysis-and-broll.md#fixed-existing-corpus-diagnostics). Preserve intended-script discrepancies and both clock refusals; the four accepted estimated proposals still require listening and timing review. Intended generation text supplies neither human ground truth nor approved captions.

Produce `pipeline/caption-style-plan.json`: profile ID/version, language, source alignment artifact, phrase/emphasis decisions, target formats, limitations and review frames. For translation, preserve meaning and source certainty; keep names/units correct and revise layout for expansion. Bilingual captions require separately timed/translated content; current registry does not automatically translate or align it.

A narrated film in another language keeps one composition: author beats, camera and cues once on the source voice, then map the new voice onto them with time-warp knots placed on words that mean the same thing (word order moves: the dropdown opens on the word for "Pro", not at the line start). Keep authoring-seconds per film-second between about 0.5 and 1.4, or moves visibly crawl or rush; let a payoff start earlier in a longer sentence instead. Captions run on film time. A voice swap is a knot rewrite. Match each language's integrated loudness to the source mix last (a denser voice came out 5 LU louder). Source: onetake (`design-reference/sources/onetake.md`).

Preview exact first/last word boundaries and pauses. Review safe zones, Cyrillic glyphs, proof labels and contrast. Burned captions and clean deliverables are separate choices; SRT/VTT export and audio loudness are handled by the repository's dedicated stages, not inferred from template selection.

For separately supplied bilingual or speaker sidecars in the final package, follow [explicit subtitle-track delivery](../../../docs/delivery.md). Extend a complete delivery request with `timeline: true` and ordered `subtitleTracks` entries (`trackId`, supplied `language`, portable `filename`, `format`); omit legacy `subtitles`. Select only existing captions/voiceover/presenter owners. Keep the snapshot's journal revision/hash, ordered selection and cue/output pins current through release. Distinct owners may overlap in time because each exports separately. A declared language or exported sidecar does not establish translation, speaker identity, timing accuracy or burned-caption readability; review those independently.

## New speech and dubbing timing

Read [audio integrations](../../../docs/audio-integrations.md) for timestamped VoiceRef/dialogue takes and dubbing. Reuse valid canonical source-bound character-derived word sidecars; do not pay to align an already valid take again. Derived isolation/voice conversion and translated performances need their own timing review. Provider dubbing VTT follows source speech, so it is not target word timing. Use explicit reviewed spans through `dubbing:conform` for template scenes, then align the actual MP3s. `localize-review` accepts the selected binding and rejects stale/wrong-language/unrelated audio; the fluent reviewer still owns meaning and reading quality.
