# Directing and performance

Read the approved brief, relevant project artefacts and `AGENTS.md` first. Use this skill when its decision is needed; do not invoke every craft skill for a simple edit. Keep leaf-agent work at depth one. Existing approval and no-spend instructions take precedence.

## Decision and output

Produce `performance-direction` in `pipeline/craft/directing-and-performance.json` (or the owning stage artefact when its schema already covers these fields): beat intention, addressee, subtext, blocking, delivery arc, verbatim dialogue, pauses, coverage and continuity constraints. Distinguish observed facts, artistic choices and unresolved assumptions. Preserve approved source/version pins.

Express the decision in the owning brief/script/design/storyboard artefact before generating assets. Tools and generation permissions come from the creative-studio router.

## Worked decisions

- The presenter starts curious, pauses on the surprising number and resolves with a calm demonstration.
- A product action is shown in one readable take before a reaction cut.

Failure to avoid: An expressive face and random camera movement distract from a precise explanation.

## Alternatives and review

Use restrained delivery for evidence; stronger contrast for a short hook. Native audio and TTS are separate ownership modes; never layer two voices.

Review: Does every decision serve the brief? Is there concrete evidence for technical/factual claims? Are timing, continuity, rights and cost assumptions explicit? Can another agent reproduce or revise the decision from the recorded artefact? Use [evaluation briefs](../creative-studio/references/evaluation.md) for review; heuristic scores do not prove audience response.
