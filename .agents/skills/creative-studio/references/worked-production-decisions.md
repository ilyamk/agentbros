# Four supplied-input production decisions

Read only the scenario being produced. These examples illustrate reasoning, not universal shot lengths, validated audience response or approval declarations. All numeric timings below are proposed targets; actual source words and source clock govern the edit. Record choice, evidence, rejected alternative, uncertainty and repair in the owning stage or pinned craft evaluation decision. Keep factual claims separate from visual direction.

## Product advertisement: demonstrate before asking

Brief assumption: the creator supplies the real packshot, an uninterrupted task demonstration, licence/claim evidence and voice/music. Proposed 20-second treatment: 0–3s show the failed task; 3–7s introduce the exact product; 7–14s perform the same task with a visible result; 14–17s hold the result and qualified benefit; 17–20s one actionable CTA. Do not infer a speed/health/savings claim from a synthetic demo.

Decision: keep the camera axis and screen coordinates consistent between the failure and result so the viewer can compare the action. A detail insert is motivated by the product interaction, not a decorative orbit. Use `scene.product-walkthrough` when the real supplied demonstration and steps match its props; inspect the registry via `pnpm video:recipe --action list`. Otherwise choose an existing proof scene. Keep original label/logo readable and do not regenerate it to make a layout fit. An asset requested from the harness remains awaiting-import until its original/result pins exist.

Rejected alternative: a dramatic AI hero image and unsupported “twice as fast” headline. Targeted repair: restore the supplied packshot, remove the numerical claim or attach valid claim evidence, extend the proof hold, then check small-screen/crop readability. Dialogue remains the mix priority; a click accent marks the actual action rather than hiding speech. Read the product workflow guide, plan with `pnpm video:workflow --scenario product-ad --request REQUEST.json`, and keep `--timeline` for the emitted authoritative edit. Plan failure is missing evidence to resolve, not permission to fetch/generate it.

## Education: keep the mechanism and caveat together

Brief assumption: supplied source establishes “part divided by nonzero whole”; a creator supplies narration timings and visual examples. Open on “Why is 2 out of 4 one half?” Show the two selected elements, then the division, then a new 3/6 example before the recap. The zero-denominator qualification belongs in the explanation and source claim map, not solely in a credits file.

Decision: use `scene.worked-example` to reveal input → operation → result in the order the narration names them. Hold the denominator while naming its constraint; don't cut to unrelated B-roll at the point of reasoning. Chapter boundaries land after a complete argument and retain word/caption mappings. Choose a neutral palette and annotate illustrative datasets as illustrations. Use `caption.bilingual` only when both language strings fit the supported limits and the secondary line does not compete with the calculation; actual glyph coverage and browser QA decide readiness.

Rejected alternative: rush three formulas into one beat to meet a nominal duration. Repair by splitting the explanation or holding longer, supplying corresponding real narration/caption timings, and checking that the recap retains the qualification. Read education/workflow direction; use `pnpm video:workflow --scenario education --request REQUEST.json`. Its plan cannot verify that supplied source assertions are true; retain source/fact review. For longer delivery, follow chapter/cache instructions and inspect full output audio plus chapter joins rather than treating one thumbnail as acceptance.

## Recording recut: preserve what changes the meaning

Brief assumption: source says “It works in this test, but not when the denominator is zero.” The source-bound transcript marks the statement, negation and caveat. A false start earlier may be removed, but removing “not” or the second clause reverses the claim. Lexical/semantic search finds candidates; it does not approve a deletion.

Decision: preserve the full qualification in the chronological keep range. Move a cut into genuine silence rather than truncating the first phoneme. Keep original audio continuous across a motivated B-roll picture cut; J/L edits must use independent audio coordinates and reviewed track-specific commands. For VFR/29.97, decoded original frame indices and the reviewed version-2 normalization map determine cuts; never multiply timestamps by 30 to invent original frames. Supply local ASR candidates only when an installed engine and pinned model were actually probed; uncertain words remain review-required.

Rejected alternative: delete a long pause plus its adjacent caveat because silence analysis calls it low energy. Targeted repair: restore protected words/context, recompute the source-bound edit hash, obtain review for the changed selection, preview at the expected timeline revision, and inspect the complete retained sentence at normal speed. `pnpm video:workflow --scenario recording-edit --request REQUEST.json` plans only. After creation, `pnpm review:ui --project ID` can apply revision-checked structured edits/undo; UI take selection is a comparison state, not a production approval. Preserve the original and the before/after evidence.

## Shorts series: make each excerpt independently honest

Brief assumption: the supplied long recording contains a definition, its worked example and a later exception. Select one question per episode; include the necessary antecedent and qualification even if an excerpt becomes longer. An episode may borrow approved context only through explicit mapped selections. Episodes have independent canonical outputs while sharing pinned originals, captions and rights lineage.

Decision: begin the educational excerpt on the question, not a context-free “yes”; end after the worked answer. Choose a format crop that retains the relevant visual and face; repeated crops of a source don't prove face tracking. Use `scene.editorial-evidence` for a source-backed statement/interpretation where appropriate, keeping attribution visible. Do not decorate every pause with a cut. If bilingual captions need more space, simplify on-screen ornament or extend the hold; do not shorten the spoken caveat to fit text.

Rejected alternative: use a provocative incomplete sentence as a viral hook and assume a pinned series approval covers new localization. Repair by deriving a newly reviewed series ID when selections/content change, checking source/context hashes and authentic speech for each episode. `pnpm video:workflow --scenario shorts-series --request REQUEST.json` plans first; final delivery is separate per episode. Supplied performance metrics remain observations with source/date, never inferred causal improvement.

## Tool and evidence handoff

- Reuse/import: [harness originals](../../harness-asset-import/SKILL.md), immutable result/provenance and entity cards. Read capabilities from observed/supplied runtime evidence; no hardcoded entitlement or hidden paid fallback.
- Sources: [resources](../../resource-discovery/SKILL.md); `pnpm resources --action route --task educational-archive` is offline routing, search/acquisition is a separate authorised operation. NASA is not universally CC0; licence evidence and placement-specific rights still matter. A trending song is a manual platform-rights step.
- Montage: [recording recut](../../recording-recut/SKILL.md), [editorial storytelling](../../editorial-storytelling/SKILL.md), [caption localization](../../captions-and-localization/SKILL.md); revision preview/apply/undo preserves original media.
- Compare: read [evaluation](evaluation.md), discover `pnpm craft:evaluate --action rubric`, pin the same inputs for baseline/candidate and retain unresolved decisions and targeted repairs. Synthetic or agent observations do not authenticate human review. Review workspace and imported delivery evidence do not grant release approval.
