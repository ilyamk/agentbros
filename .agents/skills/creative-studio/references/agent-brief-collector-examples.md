# brief-collector: worked examples

"Lumen" is an invented note-taking app that keeps exact quotes from connected sources.

## What to gather: bad and good answers

| Item | Bad | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | "Show that the product is smart and premium" | "A person types a name and Lumen shows where they met, with the exact quote and date" |
| States | "The app's interface" | "Empty window; import running; person page with a quote; search with no result, the gap shown" |
| Real data | "Some numbers" | "Years of messages, synthetic; the person page can be captured; no real people on screen" |
| Reference | "Something like Apple" | "Reference X: how speed tells the story. Do not take its colour, money theme or timer" |
| Formats | "For social" | "Vertical for Reels and landscape for the site, 30 s" |
| Budget | | "No paid APIs" or "up to $20" |
| May not be claimed | | "Do not name partner assistants; show as available only what is marked working" |
| Goal and audience | "Wide audience, learn more" | "A founder with years of correspondence; download the app" |

## Questions

Bad (costs a round, could be sent to anyone):

> What mood should the video have? Inspiring or serious? Tell me about the product.

Good (shows the material was read):

> I read PRODUCT.md and VALUE.md. Four questions remain:
> 1. Which states must be in the film? I see the person page and the goal map; anything else?
> 2. Is there a build on synthetic data we can capture? PRODUCT.md §5 describes the example only as a spec.
> 3. Which film sets the bar, and what must we not take from it?
> 4. Where will it run, and what is the call to action: download, or leave a request?
> I take the budget as zero and the word limits from VALUE.md §11 unless you correct me.

Bad: inventing the product's reason to exist ("Main emotion: confidence") when the user never said it. Good: quoting them ("calm, no shouting") in `tone`.

## Sources read: coverage in brief.md

Every path in `product-sources.json`, one line each:

> **Sources.** PRODUCT.md, VALUE.md: read fully. STATUS.md: partly, to §6 of 9; §7–9 cover the server only. design/screens.pdf: not read, the file is an image scan; the screens it names are asked about in question 2.

Bad: "Read the main documents." It hides which product the brief rests on and what a later step must still open.
