# creative-director: worked examples

Products here are invented. "Tally" is a fictional smart bike lock.

## Notes that earn a score

| Axis | Bad note | Good note |
|---|---|---|
| picture | "Strong visual" | "4: one bike with no chain in a street of chained bikes; retellable in a sentence. Not 5: the line 'never chained again' carries the argument, not the picture" |
| distinctiveness | "Unique" | "4: swapped in 'a U-lock'; the chirp at the turn breaks, the opening street does not" |
| truth | "Fine" | "3: implies theft alerts reach the owner instantly; truth marks push alerts in-development. Cuttable: the chirp alone is working" |
| feasibility | "Doable" | "4: street plate from `blender-plate`; the chirp needs a sound event and a 6-frame flash, no block, ordinary build" |

## Pairwise, both orders

Write two entries per pair: `{first: "I3", second: "I7", winner: "I3"}` and `{first: "I7", second: "I3", winner: "I3"}`. If, reading I7 first, you prefer I7, write that: the disagreement is the point. creative:rank drops it as position bias rather than letting the order decide.

## An argument against the top

Good: "Against I3: the turn depends on a sound the viewer on mute never hears; on a sound-off feed the film ends at the street shot." Bad: "Could be stronger."

## Notes for the authors, when nothing passes

Good: "No idea shows the product working before second 6"; "four of fourteen ideas imply alerts reach the owner; truth says in-development". Bad: "Be bolder"; "I liked I4": the authors cannot see the ids.

## Boards

A board frame is a library block with this idea's props: `type-hero-word` with the idea's opening word, not its demo copy; a `ui-*` block showing the product's real state. Caption in one line: "Hook: one bike, no chain, owner walking out of frame". If no block can show the hook frame, say so in `concept.md`; do not substitute a nicer frame from another idea.

## Same image, different words

"A desk lamp left on over an unanswered letter" and "a lit desk at night, a letter unopened": one subject, one scale, one world. Write `"sameImage": [["I2", "I9"]]` and, in both `distinctiveness` notes, "same picture as I9: lit desk, unopened letter". Not a pair: a lit desk at night and a postbox at dawn, even if both are about waiting for a reply.

## Score once

Bad: rank says below threshold; you lower I4's `hook` by a point, or raise another item to make it pass. Good: the review stays as scored; your report says "I4 hook may be generous: the opening frame needs sound to read". Rank refused a missing pair: add that pair, change nothing else.

## A board that contradicts its score

The hook board for the top idea is a chat bubble on a flat ground; at 360 px wide its text is unreadable and nothing in the frame stops a thumb, although `picture` scored 4. In `concept.md`: "The hook frame does not read at phone size: small grey text, no subject. In its place I offer the fourth-ranked idea: a phone face-down on a table that lights up once." The scores and `concept.json` do not change; the user decides.
