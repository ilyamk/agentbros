# Executable source-backed lesson workflow

Use `runEducationWorkflow` for supplied-media lessons. Read `docs/workflows/education.md` and the strict education schema. Assemble concrete six-stage chapters, not generic headings: establish prerequisites, pose a useful question, explain one mechanism, show a worked example with assumptions, ask for application and give a self-contained recap.

Pin the original evidence and media hashes. Attach each claim to an exact source excerpt and locator. Explicitly label inference and uncertainty. Carry scope/assumptions verbatim into both the explanation and recap. Do not call a checksum/excerpt check factual review. Do not invent narration, sources, licences, approvals or prices. Import existing harness outputs rather than regenerate them.

Plan first without writes. Check authored captions against spoken script and source time windows. Create a new immutable-original draft; then use revisioned timeline edits and chapter-local caching. Review actual narration, numerical examples, diagram readability, caption safe zones and every output format. Preserve caveats in any extracted recap/short. Finish through existing QA and delivery gates; synthetic browser fixtures cannot approve a real lesson.

When supplied narration already has a local index with explicit native word evidence, the [estimated word proposal route](../../../../docs/offline-word-timing.md) can assist review: `pnpm media:words --project ID --index INDEX.json --output public/projects/ID/pipeline/words-proposal.json`. Its original seconds and raw engine rows are estimates; verify names, numbers, units, negations and boundaries against the recording and approved script. Do not alter a sourced claim to match an ASR error. Keep the immutable source/evidence/model/runtime pins, correct the reviewed transcript separately, and map it through the lesson's actual narration offsets. This creates no education request, approval or verified forced alignment.
