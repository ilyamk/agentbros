# Prepare audio before the mix

Use stems to isolate drums for rhythm analysis or balance permitted musical parts; denoise speech before compression, cue placement and mixing. Keep the immutable source, source rights and the processed asset's endpoint, operation ID and reference provenance. Separation does not grant rights to reuse a recording or its stems. A voice change also needs permission for the source recording and target voice.

Local `pnpm audio:stems` uses Demucs. Verified fal stems, denoise and voice-change recipes are in [the catalogue guide](../../../../docs/fal-catalogue.md#audio-to-audio-recipes); run `pnpm generate:model` with `--dry-run` before authorised paid work. Measure actual processed timing and listen for lost speech or transients before replacing mix inputs. Preserve the original as an alternative. Contracts and meters do not establish listening approval.
