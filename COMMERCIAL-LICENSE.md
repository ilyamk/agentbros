## Who needs one

You need a commercial licence for any production use that the Additional Use Grant in LICENSE does not cover. In particular:

- **An organisation with more than three (3) Personnel** that makes production use of Agent Bros. Pictures. Personnel are counted by head, not by full-time equivalent, at the time of use, across the organisation and all its Affiliates, and include employees, officers, directors, owners who work in the business, contractors, freelancers, interns and agency workers.
- **Anyone, whatever their size,** whose production use includes offering Agent Bros. Pictures, or a modified version or derivative work of it, to third parties as a hosted, managed or embedded service, API or platform that gives them access to its functionality.
- **Anyone, whatever their size,** whose production use includes distributing, sublicensing, selling or reselling Agent Bros. Pictures, or a modified version or derivative work of it, as part of or bundled with a product or service offered to third parties.

In this guide, production use means using the software for real work rather than to evaluate, test, develop or study it: for example, making films or other media that are published, delivered or used in your business, or running it as part of your business operations. Copying, modifying and redistributing the source code, and non-production use, are granted to everyone by the Business Source License and need no commercial licence; the conditions above limit only the free production-use grant.

## Who does not

- **The free production grant.** You may make production use free of charge if you are (a) an individual using it on your own behalf, including to produce films and other media for your clients; (b) a Small Organisation, meaning a legal entity that, together with its Affiliates, has no more than three (3) Personnel; or (c) a non-profit organisation, or an accredited educational or research institution, using it for its non-commercial activities. In each case you must not offer it as a hosted, managed or embedded service, API or platform to third parties, or distribute, sublicense, sell or bundle it in a product or service offered to third parties.
- **Non-production use, at any size.** Everyone may copy, modify, create derivative works of, redistribute and make non-production use of the software: evaluation, testing, development and study. A company of any size can try it without a commercial licence until it uses it in production.
- **Contributing.** Preparing and submitting contributions under the [CLA](CLA.md) is permitted to everyone.
- **Rendered films.** The licence governs the software, not the films and other media made with it. Delivering those films is not distribution of the software. Watching, publishing or reusing a rendered film needs no licence from the Licensor.

## What a commercial licence grants

The exact terms are set in each signed agreement. A commercial licence:

- permits the production use, and any hosting, embedding or distribution, that it names, beyond the free grant in LICENSE;
- is scoped by organisation (with or without its Affiliates) and by Personnel tier, and by the products, deployments or sites agreed;
- runs for a term (subscription) or is perpetual for the versions it names, as agreed;
- includes the right to host the software for third parties, embed it in a product or platform, or distribute it with your product, only when the agreement says so;
- may include updates, support, priority fixes, warranty or indemnity, only when the agreement says so.

Fees are agreed in each proposal; they are not published.

Its threshold and counting differ from the free grant of this licence.
- **GSAP**, under Webflow's Standard "No Charge" GSAP License ([gsap.com/standard-license](https://gsap.com/standard-license)), including its restriction on no-code visual animation builders that compete with Webflow.

## Rendered films

The Business Source License governs the software, not, in general, the films the software produces. A rendered film is still subject to the rights in the material it uses: fonts, music, footage, 3D models, voices, provider terms and the Remotion licence for the company that renders it. This is not legal advice; check your case.
