# Content series

Last updated: 2026-11-20

## Start

Plan recurring episodes with consistent identity and distinct substance. Use [campaign production](../../docs/campaign-production.md) to author the request.

```bash
pnpm campaign:production --project <id> --action plan --request <file>
pnpm campaign:production --project <id> --action init --request <file>
pnpm next --project <id>
```

## What good looks like

Viewers recognise the character and programme while each dated episode delivers its own information. Shared identity versions and episode costs remain accountable.

## Method

1. Define the recurring character, style and structural rules using approved entity versions. Pin those versions instead of relying on a mutable latest card.
2. Assign episode IDs, dates, topics and source facts. Check that the month contains meaningful distinct episodes rather than cosmetic permutations.
3. Separate shared character/branding assets from episode material and show their ownership in the allocation and reuse plan.
4. Let specialists author the episode-specific scripts and performances through their existing child routes. New dialogue always requires its own timing.
5. Inspect continuity across the batch and review weak episodes individually. A changed recurring identity requires a reviewed campaign revision.

For a series of product tutorials, the opt-in [series kit](../../docs/experiments.md#series-kit) pins shared titles, captions, cursor and cues when the campaign prepares its film members.

## Tools

Use world-and-entity-bible for identity, copywriter for episode structure and campaign:next for dispatch. Shared material imports preserve provenance while review:ui supports continuity comparisons.

Approve representative episodes before extending the schedule. Pin recurring identities, reject duplicate authored substance and retain dated episode evidence. Continuity feedback names the affected scene and acceptance criterion.

## Anti-patterns

Do not promise automatic publication from scheduledFor dates, reuse yesterday’s alignment for new dialogue or accept an entire month from one representative episode.
