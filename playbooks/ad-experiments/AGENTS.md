# Advertising experiments

Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Start

Create a controlled creative experiment around a supplied product film. Use [campaign production](../../docs/campaign-production.md) to author the request.

```bash
pnpm campaign:production --project <id> --action plan --request <file>
pnpm campaign:production --project <id> --action init --request <file>
pnpm next --project <id>
```

## What good looks like

The proposed openings differ in a named persuasive mechanism. Product proof, disclosures and the control body remain traceable, and each comparison has a hypothesis.

## Method

1. Identify the control and mark mutable hooks, arguments, offers and endings. Freeze everything required for a fair comparison.
2. Select explicit combinations with campaign-producer. Pair alternatives deliberately; avoid an uncontrolled multiplication of factors.
3. Author child configurations or direct film specialists to replace only the selected material. Record the retained media in reusePlan.
4. Review the creative plan, then advance campaign:next. Obtain independent criticism of every rendered treatment before offering the final package.
5. Associate supplied campaign metrics with exact variant hashes and cohorts; do not infer an advertising winner from visual preference.

## Tools

Use copywriter for openings and CTA alternatives, models:pick for missing media, and review:ui to compare treatments. Refresh an exhausted creative by declaring the successful sections as controls.

Select a pilot spanning the control and changed hooks. Declare variable factors and editable scene IDs; preserve the other arguments. Route concrete timing feedback to fixer before an independent recheck.

## Anti-patterns

Do not invent stronger product claims, alter several hidden variables or treat view counts from different cohorts as causal proof.
