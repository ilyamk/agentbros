# Autonomy and the guard

Part of the [production chain](../production-chain.md). Read when the user asks the harness to go on by itself, and at a `present-best` stop.

Interactive is the default: without `pipeline/policy.json` every gate asks the user exactly as before. A user who wants the film made without being asked at each step grants a **policy** in their own words; the harness then passes a few named gates for them, records every such decision in a ledger they can revoke, and stops at limits that hold for every project.

## The policy

`pipeline/policy.json` (`ProjectPolicySchema` v1, `src/types/policy.ts`) is its own file: not in brief.json, not a step input, so writing it reopens nothing. Revisions are appended; the last is in force.

```bash
pnpm film:policy --project <id> --autonomy autonomous --answer "<the user's words>" \
  --ceiling-usd 5 --providers fal --auto-pass concept,script --spend-usd 3 \
  --pre-answer musicRoute=silence --pre-answer "silenceReason=<their reason>"
pnpm film:policy --project <id> --show
```

- `autonomy`: `interactive` or `autonomous`. `grantedBy` keeps the user's words and when.
- `budget`: `ceilingUsd`, `maxJobUsd`, `allowedProviders`, `allowUserBoundQuotes` (default false). It binds OperationStore: `configure` refuses a ceiling or per-job limit above the policy's, `reserveOperation` refuses a provider outside the list, a reservation past the policy's ceiling, and under autonomy a hand-bound (`user-bound`) quote. Granting a budget also installs or raises the store's ceiling up to it, on the same words.
- `preAnswers`: `filmType`, `formats`, `proposition`, `musicRoute` (`silence` with `silenceReason`, or `user-supplied` with `musicFile`, the shortlist id of their track), `swaps` (a replica: each swap's `what`), and on a recording `captionStyle` (a caption profile id) and `shorts` (a count, 2 to 30). film:policy refuses with INVALID_INPUT a value that is not an option or a key that does not fit the project (`src/lib/autonomy/pre-answers.ts`).
- `autoPass`: `concept`, `script`, `replica-spec`, `material-free`, `shorts`.
- `spendUsd`: paid material the policy may approve by itself (below); `--spend-usd 0` removes it.
- `finalReviewOnly`, and `limits` (below): only the limits the user set are stored; the mode's defaults fill the rest.

A policy covers a bespoke film or a recording's source project (footage:next; a recut id resolves to it). Anything that widens what runs without asking (autonomy, a higher budget, a new provider, user-bound quotes, a pre-answer, an auto-pass step, a raised limit, a new or higher `spendUsd`) needs `--answer` with the user's words; lowering or removing needs none. MCP has no tool for it.

## What a policy may pass

Allowlist only (`src/lib/film/autonomy.ts`); film:next turns a covered gate into `run` `pnpm film:approve --project <id> --step <s> --policy --expect <sha256>`, which recomputes the basis, refuses when the judged hash changed, records the approval as proxy `user-policy` and appends the decision.

A pre-answer the options no longer hold (an unknown caption profile in an older policy, fewer clips proposed than `shorts`, a spec with other swaps) is ignored: the user is asked, with the reason in `advisories`. Without an autonomous policy footage:next and film:next ask as before.

Spend: an approval under the policy never counts as the user's (`userApprovalEvents` drops every proxy). Spending on material is authorised (`spendApprovalEvents`: `material-sourced` and the step lease's `paidAllowed`) by the user's own yes or by a standing policy decision of kind `spend`; a judge or pre-answer approval and a benchmark proxy never authorise it. Each generation still passes OperationStore admission (ceiling, per-job limit, providers, no hand-bound quote under autonomy): autonomy never bypasses the budget.

## The decision ledger

`pipeline/decisions.jsonl`, append-only: `{id, step, kind: preAnswer|judge|spend, artefactHashes, evidence, judgeVerdict, amountUsd?, scores?, alternatives?, policyRevision, at}`; a `spend` decision carries `amountUsd` and `evidence.items` (`id=bound,...`). The human step's question lists the standing decisions by step. To undo one:

```bash
pnpm film:revert --project <id> --decision d-001 --answer "<the user's words>"
```

It appends a `revoked` line; the approval stops counting, the step's gate is open again and is never passed again by the policy on the same artefacts, and the hash cascade reopens what came after. A revert is a re-entry of that step for the guard. A revoked spend decision stops authorising spending but still counts toward `spendUsd`: what it authorised may already be bought. Autonomous decisions live in the project ledger, never in `memory/`.

On a recording (steps `footage-captions`, `footage-shorts` in the source project's ledger): a revoked caption style is dropped from the goals while they still hold it, and the policy never picks that style again; a revoked shorts approval makes footage:next ask the user again, and the policy never picks again on the same proposals. Limits: the series request stays on disk until the user's own `shorts:request` replaces it, episode projects already created stay until `pnpm project:remove`, and a caption style already applied to the recut's timeline stays until the user's choice is applied over it.

## The guard

Every project, with or without a policy, has limits (`GlobalLimitsSchema`). Defaults by mode (2026-11-17): interactive (no autonomous policy, `INTERACTIVE_LIMITS`) `maxTotalRounds` 60 and `maxActiveHours` 12; autonomous (`DEFAULT_LIMITS`) 30 and 4; both `maxStepRounds` 6, `maxReentries` 2, `maxProviderSpendUsd` (unset: the policy's ceiling, else only the store's), `maxAgentTokens` (advisory). A limit the user set in the policy wins in either mode; switching mode needs no words for the defaults alone. `pipeline/guard.jsonl` is appended by the commands that do work: film:brief a dispatch (a re-printed brief of the same round is not counted), film:done a seal and, when a sealed step's input had changed, a re-entry, film:approve an approval, `feedback --confirm` and film:revert a re-entry, film:policy a raise. Spend is read live from `operations.json`.

- A step's rounds: its most-dispatched role and lane. Total rounds: the sum over steps.
- Active hours: the gaps between logged events, each gap over 30 minutes left out.
- Counts come only from the log: deleting critiques or receipts lowers nothing, and nothing resets it.

When a round limit is reached, film:next stops instead of dispatching (a seal or a question still runs) and film:brief refuses; a re-entry, hour or spend limit stops everything. The stop is `step: present-best` with `limits`, `best` (the best kept version from `out/<id>/versions/`, its measure and critic total, failing legs), `unresolved`, `spend` and `raise`: show the user the best version and ask. Raising needs their words: `pnpm film:policy --project <id> --raise maxTotalRounds=40 --answer "<the user's words>"`.
