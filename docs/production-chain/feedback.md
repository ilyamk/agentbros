# The user's feedback

Part of the [production chain](../production-chain.md); read at the `human` step.

When the user watches the film and asks for changes, their words become a step input, not a note. Write their items to a JSON file (`{"verdict": "revise", "items": [{"quote": "<their words, verbatim>", "aspect": "music", "severity": "must", "at": {"fromSec": 0, "toSec": 8}}]}`) and run `pnpm feedback --project <id> --file <items.json>`: a preview of where each item goes (`routedTo`), which steps reopen and which owners redo work, with warnings when paid material reopens. Show it to the user; on their yes, run it again with `--confirm`.

- Code routes each `aspect` (`src/lib/feedback/route.ts`): audience to strategy, concept to the concept teams (`ideas`), the script's words (`hook-line`, `words`, `ending-cta`) to the script writers (`script-variants`; a replica's to replica-spec), opening-picture, pacing and length to beat-sheet, claim to truth, music to music, voice to narration (or sound), sfx-mix to sound, look to look, shot, captions and framing to build, material to material (or build). A step the film's type does not run and `unclear` go to `ask`.
- Feedback to `ideas` or `script-variants`, or any upstream change that reaches them (audience to strategy, claim to truth, a different track), reopens their blind pool: `film:next` dispatches every team or variant, and the first `film:brief` records a new round in `pipeline/rounds/<kind>-reopens.json` (the next round after a reviewed one). Each brief carries the user's quotes as a constraint (with upstream feedback's) and the previous winner as what the user rejected (or, after an upstream change, what was written before it). Earlier rounds stay in `pipeline/rounds/`; a new creative director judges the new pool, review limits count from the reopened round, and the pick reruns for the user's gate. Feedback reopens a pool at most twice before `film:next` asks the user what they picture; `--clarify` lets it go on.
- `--confirm` writes `pipeline/feedback/fb-NNN.json` (`FeedbackSchema`: bound to the watched render's hash, source always the user) and `pipeline/feedback/open/<step>.json`, a tracked input of that step: the receipt hashes reopen it and everything downstream, and `film:next` returns the earliest one. The quotes go to the spine, so the owner's brief shows them.
- Sealing the step with changed outputs marks its items `addressed` and clears the open file; `film:done --unchanged-because` marks them `declined` with the reason. The same aspect sent to the same step twice makes `film:next` ask the user; `pnpm feedback --step <s> --clarify "<their words>"` records the answer.
- A confirmed `revise` on the render the user accepted withdraws that acceptance: once the reopened steps close again (or nothing reopened), `film:next` asks for their verdict again.
- The confirmed answer carries a `capture` hint for a lesson, with `--step` and `--axis` filled and `--quote` left for the user's words.
- Placeholder quotes are refused. Recordings, campaigns: [other routes](../feedback-routes.md).
