# Creative department

Steps strategy to script of the [production chain](../production-chain.md): read at those steps.

Steps 4 to 7 and 10 to 12 work like an agency's creative department. Data in `src/lib/film/chain.ts`; artefacts in `src/types/studio.ts`; logic in `src/lib/film/studio.ts`.

1. **Strategy.** The strategist writes `strategy.json` and a one-page `strategy.md` in the user's language: audience and their moment, platform and sound on or off, one proposition, the first 2 s hook mechanic, tone, mandatories, forbidden, success criteria and the film type (which must repeat the brief's). `creativeTeams` picks 2 to 4 stances; the default is metaphor, product-in-use, provocation. With product truth, its brief names two optional fields: `strongestAlternative {name, whyConsidered}` (what the buyer would otherwise do that looks most like the product on screen) and `productOnlyProofs[] {proof, claim, looksLike}`; each proof's `claim` quotes a product-truth claim's text verbatim (or its id), and the step is not done unless that claim's status is `working`. User gate.
2. **Teams.** `film:next` returns one dispatch per team: `film:brief --step ideas --team <stance>`. Each team (concept-director with that stance, described in `.agents/skills/creative-studio/references/creative-teams.md`) writes `pipeline/concepts/<team>.json`: 4 to 6 ideas for the current round. With `pipeline/category-scan.json`, the brief adds "Category conventions and clichés" (clichés, dontTake verbatim, whiteSpace) and the team's method: kill-first-10 from the clichés into `killedObvious` (10), then one method per team (metaphor zwicky, product-in-use scamper, provocation triz, human-moment synectics, others random-entry), whose stimuli film:brief writes to `pipeline/stimuli/<method>-<project>_<team>.json` at seed `<project>:<team>`. At least 2 ideas carry `stimulus {method, seed, prompt}`, or the team's file is invalid. An idea that turns a cliché may say so in `subverts {cliche, how}`. When the strategy has `productOnlyProofs`, the brief adds "Proof only this product can show" (the strongest alternative, each proof with its frame), and every idea shows a proof in its hook or turn and records `proofFrame {proof, where: hook|turn}` naming one of the strategy's proofs, or the team's file is invalid. `creative:pool` keeps `stimulus`, `subverts`, `proofFrame` and `killedObvious` in the key, never in the pool: the director judges what is written, never against the category list. Its brief (and the director's) lists the existing material from product-truth `material[]` and the library catalogue (`docs/library/catalogue.json`, read but untracked, so library work never stales the step); with budget 0 or none it says plainly that the picture is built only from library blocks and existing material.
3. **Pool.** `creative:pool --kind ideas` strips team and authorship, renumbers the ideas `I1..In` and shuffles them with a seed (default `<project>:<kind>:<round>`), writing `pipeline/pools/ideas.json` and the key `pipeline/pools/ideas-key.json`, which the director never reads. It records the idea-review receipt itself, so the next action is the director's brief; `film:brief --role critic` before that names the command to run.
4. **Review.** A new creative-director every round (`film:brief --step idea-review --role critic`) scores every idea on the project's frozen rubric (`pipeline/rubric.json`; [v2](../../.agents/skills/creative-studio/references/creative-rubric-v2.md) for new projects, [v1](../../.agents/skills/creative-studio/references/creative-rubric.md) for projects without the file or made with `film:init --rubric 1`): picture, hook, distinctiveness, truth, feasibility, ambition, native, 1 to 5 with anchored levels. It judges every pair among the six best twice, once in each order, writes an argument against the top and notes for the authors, then runs `creative:rank`, which keeps a pair only when both orders agree, ranks, and computes the verdict into `pipeline/creative-review/ideas-round-<n>.json`. A hand-written ranking or verdict that code would not write makes the step invalid. Ideas the director declares one picture (`sameImage: [["I5", "I7"]]`) never share the top: the next idea takes the place, and `ideas-differ` fails a concept that holds both. Rank records the judgement it ranked by hash, in the review and in `creative-review/judged/` (so a rewritten review is still checked); a rescore after the verdict needs `--rescore-reason`, is kept in `rescoredAfterVerdict` with its diff, and is shown in `film:status` and the concept question.
5. **Threshold.** An idea passes with every axis at 3 or more, truth at 4 or more, and a mean of 3.6 or more; a round passes when one idea does. Otherwise `film:next` sends the teams back with the notes (their briefs name the review), at most twice. A third round below the threshold, or a round whose best mean does not rise, goes to the user.
6. **Concept and boards.** creative-director runs `creative:pick --kind ideas` (concept.json: the top three, the first recommended, each with the argument against it), writes `concept-boards.json` (per idea a hook and a last frame, optionally the turn: a library block, its props and a caption) and runs `concept:boards`, which renders `pipeline/boards/<id>-<role>.jpg` and the contact sheet `pipeline/boards/boards.jpg`. The user chooses from `concept.md` and the sheet.
7. **Script variants.** After the music and its map, three script-writers write variants a (sparse, image-led), b (claim-led), c (question-led) to `pipeline/scripts/<v>.json`. `creative:pool --kind scripts` pools them blind and measures every `read` line against `plan:check`'s reading floor; an overrun caps the reading axis at 2. A new creative-director scores hook line, truth, reading, music fit and one message, in both orders. `creative:pick --kind scripts` copies the winner to `script.json`, `script-music-map.json` and `script.md`, and the runner-up to `script-alternative.json` and `.md`, which the user sees beside it.

The rubric, its anchors and the threshold are frozen in one commit ("freeze creative rubric v1"); `tests/creative-rubric.test.ts` pins each anchors file's hash, so a change is a new version. Rubric v2 keeps v1's axes, threshold and ranking and changes only the distinctiveness anchor: the swap target is the strategy's `strongestAlternative` when it has one, 4 or more needs a product-only frame at the hook or turn, and an idea that differs only by a caption, a handle or end-card copy scores 2 at most. Under v2 the director's brief adds "Swap target", naming the strongest alternative. The pool and the review record `rubricVersion`; `creative:rank` refuses a review whose version is not the project's.

**Learning.** creative-postmortem turns the user's verdicts into lessons in their local memory (`pnpm memory --add --type lesson`), generalised (never a product's name) and tagged with the rubric axis they bear on; agents' findings become observations there. `design-reference/creative-lessons.md` holds the harness's curated lessons, promoted from memory only by an approved proposal. The creative director reads both every round and cites a lesson where it moves a score.
