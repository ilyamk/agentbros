# Offline rhythm, SFX and cost-to-finish

`pnpm audio:editorial --action rhythm --input music.wav --output rhythm.json` decodes mono PCM through local FFmpeg, records a source checksum, amplitude onsets and four-second energy sections. Regularity confidence is conservative; silence/irregular material falls back to speech/story rhythm. It does not detect music genre, semantic emphasis, metre or automatically cut footage. `--overrides marks.json` accepts strictly ordered in-range beat timestamps with reviewer and rationale.

Five seeded WAV generators (`click`, `pop`, `whoosh`, `impact`, `chime`) are reproducible: `--action sfx --input spec.json --output take.wav`. Spec fields: kind, positive integer seed, durationSec (0.03–3), gain (0.001–0.8). Existing takes cannot be overwritten. Reports contain sample peak/RMS and actual FFmpeg loudness measurements. No provider is called.

Import generated or licensed stock sounds with `asset:import`. Cue specs identify id/event, owner trackId/clipId, start/end anchor, integer offsetFrames, imported assetId, durationSec and gain. `--action cues --project ID --input cues.json --output command.json` writes a reversible `add-track` batch. Plan in stdout includes the expected revision and resulting timeline. Apply `pnpm timeline:edit --project ID --command-file command.json --revision REV --dry-run`, inspect, then omit `--dry-run`. Undo restores the exact previous snapshot. Separate SFX tracks preserve simultaneous tails and speech ownership. Missing anchors, duplicate IDs and tails outside delivery fail. Existing cues are revision snapshots: after moving an owner, replan and review the cue rather than silently moving sound.

Independent visual/speech tracks already support J/L cuts via move/trim/batch edits. A visual hold never silently trims spoken wording; reviewed source-based cut proposals own speech edits.

`--action forecast --project ID --input finish-tasks.json` reads durable budget jobs and bounded quotes, computes remaining liabilities/estimates and flags unknown prices, attempt limits, pending operations, per-job/project caps and consumption of protected repair reserve. It neither reserves money nor approves reshoots. Pending provider jobs and missing invoice evidence remain visible. Use the shared execution service for the eventual request.

Verification: `pnpm exec tsx --test tests/editorial-audio.test.ts` synthesizes/decodes all five sounds, analyzes real silence and pulses, checks frame-anchored cues and budget headroom without network calls.

`pnpm sfx:generate --project ID --prompt "Dry paper fold" --duration 2 --endpoint elevenlabs/sound-effects/v3 --output public/projects/ID/assets/sfx/paper.mp3 --dry-run` plans a durable fal audio take without spending or writing. For foley, use `--video FILE` with `mirelo-ai/sfx2.0/video-to-audio` (local video up to 8 MiB, at least one second; optional prompt, one SFX-only variation, WAV output). Text SFX also supports `fal-ai/stable-audio-3/small/sfx/text-to-audio`. Review the quote and durable budget, obtain consent, then execute with the same arguments without `--dry-run`, adding an authorised `--max-cost-usd` when the price is unknown. Reuse `--operation-id` to resume; never create a new paid operation to recover an ambiguous submit. Publication registers an immutable project asset with endpoint/job provenance and writes `OUTPUT.take.json`; use its `src` as the cue/mix file. No take enters the shared SFX library, and ElevenLabs library recipes remain `needs-generation` until separately authorised generation. Music uses `generate:music`, not this sound-only command.
