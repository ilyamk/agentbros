# Film types

Generated from `src/lib/film/routes.ts` by `pnpm film:route --write-doc`; do not edit by hand. The type is the film's technique, named by its playbook (the kind of video: [playbooks](../playbooks/AGENTS.md)) and set with `pnpm film:init --type <type>`: it switches chain steps on or off and hands each step its tools. Owners and their inputs and outputs come from the chain (`src/lib/film/chain.ts`); rules from `STEP_RULES`.

| Type | Choose it for | Material | Steps off |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`motion-graphics`](film-types/motion-graphics.md) | an animated product or promo film built from type, shapes, UI and 3D, with no outside footage | none: library, code and 3D | narration, material |
| [`ui-demo`](film-types/ui-demo.md) | the real product's screens in use: a web or mobile app recorded and staged at reading scale | captures of the real product's screens, recorded frame by frame | none |
| [`footage`](film-types/footage.md) | live footage or b-roll, stock or supplied, cut to music with type over picture | licensed stock or supplied clips, chosen as selects with in and out points | narration |
| [`ai-video`](film-types/ai-video.md) | shots generated by video models, directed shot by shot, within an authorised budget | generated stills and video shots, each with model, prompt, operation id and cost | none |
| [`talking-head`](film-types/talking-head.md) | a presenter or UGC creator speaking to camera, recorded, with captions and inserts | the presenter's recorded takes, with release, plus any inserts | narration |
| [`explainer`](film-types/explainer.md) | teaching an idea with diagrams, data and worked examples, usually with a voice | none: library, code and 3D | material |
| [`collage`](film-types/collage.md) | mixed media: cut-out photos, paper, texture and type assembled on a flat field | photos and textures cut out, with licence, plus any generated pieces | narration |
| [`replica`](film-types/replica.md) | an exact copy of one given film, shot for shot and frame for frame, with only what the user names swapped (only when they ask to copy a film, not for a film in its style) | the user's replacements for what another owner holds: their real product's screens, logos, copy assets and licensed media, each a swap in the spec | truth, reference, strategy, discovery, ideas, idea-review, concept, script-variants, script-review, script, narration, look, beat-sheet, animatic, measure, critic |

A hybrid sets `secondaryFilmType`: its material switches the material step on and its tools join the primary type's. A brief with no type is routed as `motion-graphics`, and `film:next` says so.
