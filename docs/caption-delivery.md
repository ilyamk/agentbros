# Subtitle and clean deliverables

`pnpm captions:export --project ID --output out/ID/captions.srt [--timeline] [--track TRACK] [--format vtt] [--profile profile.json] [--dry-run]` derives subtitle timestamps from the same exact TimelineIR source/output mapping used by rendering. Splits merge contiguous fragments of the same word; negations and source wording remain verbatim. Ambiguous overlapping owners and overflowing unbreakable words fail explicitly. Profiles set maximum line length, line count, word count, cue duration and gap; text is never translated implicitly. Milliseconds are rounded only at SRT/VTT delivery.

`pnpm render --project ID --captions off` writes `{format}-clean.mp4`; the ordinary captioned filename is preserved. Caption suppression affects the captions layer only. Presenter speech and audio master routing are unchanged. Exporting a subtitle file does not prove semantic or rights approval.

Local verification: `pnpm exec tsx --test tests/caption-delivery.test.ts tests/render-plan.test.ts`; browser render suite runs separately with `pnpm test:browser`. No paid alignment call is made by these commands.

For an immutable final package with separately supplied bilingual or speaker tracks, use the complete [strict delivery request](delivery.md) with `timeline: true` and `subtitleTracks`. Capture its snapshot before rendering. This opt-in selection exports each existing owner separately and pins declared language, filename, cues and current journal bytes; it requires all ordinary release reviews. Do not combine it with legacy `subtitles`, infer translation or treat a sidecar as visually reviewed burned captions. Existing single-track CLI export remains available.

## Burned caption styles in timeline renders

The timeline's `captionStyle` (set with the `caption-style` edit command, see [timeline edit](timeline-edit.md)) chooses the burned profile for every caption owner: `karaoke` (default, unchanged), `phrase`, `keyword`, `bilingual`, or a `captions` library preset (`outline-pop`, `italic-shadow`, `keyword-colour`, `word-box`, `line-box`, `chunky-stack`, `editorial-mix`, `clean-sentence`, `single-punch`, `typewriter`; the older `word-highlight`, `bold-upper`, `boxed-word` and `keyword-emphasis` are drawn by their closest preset). A `captionStyle` composition prop overrides it; `render --captions off` still removes every profile. Library presets build pages with `createTikTokStyleCaptions` (1–6 words by preset, at most two lines, three for stacked and mixed pages), break pages at every speaker change and sentence end, read through an outline, a hard or soft shadow or a solid box (no blurred scrim), skip `event` words and stay inside the platform safe zone (vertical: x 140–940, y 220–1520, clear of the platform's top 220, bottom 400 and right 140 px; see [placement](captions-styles.md#placement)). `checkCaptionPages` in `src/lib/captions/readability.ts` reports lines, words, short pages, reading speed above 17 characters per second and a box outside the zone (`presetLimits` gives a preset's own limits); `checkCaptionBackground` measures every drawn colour (3:1 minimum) against the darkest and brightest footage under the caption on a clean still, with the treatment applied, for frames the caller picks. `pnpm captions:place` runs it on a clean still of every page before rendering, chooses each page's position clear of faces, printed text and the platform interface (`options.positions`, `checkCaptionPlacement`: `CAPTION_IN_UI_ZONE`, `CAPTION_OVER_FACE`) and records the mildest passing rung of the preset's legibility ladder (`options.legibility`) in the timeline, so renders are reproducible; `checkCaptionSizes` reports `TEXT_TOO_SMALL` below a cap height of 3% of the frame height (2% for the small words of a mixed page). Bilingual captions draw an opaque rounded box per line (default black, white text); a translucent `backgroundColor` is drawn opaque. Recipes and research: [caption styles](captions-styles.md). These limits are for burned short-form pages; the sidecar limits above (42 characters per line) are separate. Choosing a style: [caption styles](../.agents/skills/captions-and-localization/references/caption-styles.md).

Local verification: `pnpm exec tsx --test tests/caption-styles.test.ts`; `RUN_RENDER_TESTS=1 pnpm exec tsx --test tests/caption-profiles-browser.test.ts` renders every profile through `TimelineComposition`.
