# Audio and sync — Agent Rules

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Mixer, music analysis, edit plans, SFX and sync in `audio/`, `audio-map/`, `audio-curves/` and their `scripts/workers/`. Parent: [src/lib/AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md); craft: [sound and sync](../../../design-reference/sound-and-sync.md).

## Start here

`pnpm audio:map`, `edit:plan`, `edit:snap`, `audio:curves`.

## Rules

- **Mixer** (`audio/local-mix.ts`): music ducks under speech no deeper than `duckDb` (plan field, default −9 dB, range −24..−3; the compressor's dry share sets the floor). `measureDuck(plan)` reports the measured dip and the bed under the voice; `audio:mix` prints it as `metadata.duck`. Loudness presets: `review` −16 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP (plan default), `social` −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP for delivery.
- **Drum grid** (`scripts/workers/audio-map.py`): kick, snare and hat hits come from band-limited attacks on the drums stem (or the full mix), and tempo and phase are least-squares fitted on their sixteenths, so a syncopated kick counts. The bar's first sixteenth is where the level jumps, the drums come in, the kick lands and the snare answers on two and four. Snares on two and four vote for the beat's sixteenth, so a backbeat never lands between beats. `grid.barKnown` is false when `downbeatConfidence` is below 0.2: downbeats are then listed but are not structure, and edit planning should not treat them as such. A drums-stem fit that is `reliable` wins; a mix fit wins only where the tracker's beats sit on it. `map.grid` records the source, residual, bars and every candidate; `map.drums` the hits. A clean kick train is proof.
- **Stems** (`audio-map/stems.ts`, `scripts/workers/audio-stems.py`): CPU Demucs `htdemucs`, no shifts; both callers use the locked `stems` group. `AUDIO_STEMS_UVX` accepts a uv-compatible runner. Input-SHA cache: `.cache/audio-stems/<model>/<sha>/`, outside `PROJECT_STATE_CACHES`, removed by `clean --scope cache`. `tryStems` failure falls back to the mix (`diagnostics.stemsSkipped`).
- **Music structure** (`scripts/workers/audio-structure.py`, after the map worker): `map.structure` adds bars (energy, drum density, drums, bass, novelty); phrase starts on 4/8/16-bar lines (offset where novelty concentrates), confidence from novelty, fills, risers and level jump; sections labelled intro/build/drop/break/outro by `intensity` (half level, half drum density, each against the fullest section), with a `reason` quoting both readings and ranks; a build leads into a fuller section; fills (drum hits per beat at 1.6x the usual rate in the bar before a boundary); risers (centroid up a third of an octave or level 3 dB into a boundary, above the wobble); risers and fills at most one per four bars and a sixth of the cue, strongest kept; boundaries under half a bar apart merged, none (nor drop, riser, fill) in the last second before the stop or fade; breaks (silence, stop, drum or bass drop-out); and the ending (hard stop vs fade, final downbeat). Stems when the map has them, HPSS and a low band otherwise. It adds form lanes to the map PNG (patching `draw_row`). With `grid.barKnown` false, phrases still count bars at up to half confidence, `edit:plan` marks no cut as a downbeat (`barKnown` in its output) and plan:check's advisory asks only for beats. Validated on four references: cuts sit on beats (ref-service-explainer 12/14, chance 0.44); phrase lines and downbeat shifts unproven.
- **Edit plan** (`audio-map/edit-plan.ts`, pure): `planEdit` turns the structure into slots (`calm`: phrase and section starts; `punchy` (default) and `flurry`: section starts, drops and the stop as phrase-level lines, beat cuts inside in an uneven beat-count pattern; drops as hero hits with ground swaps, holds on breaks, flurries on fills, a stop on the final downbeat), library categories and SFX kinds per slot, and unleaded cue drafts for `edit:snap`; maps without `structure` use phrases and sections. `edit:snap` also snaps `phrase` cues to structure phrase and section starts.
- **Cue leads** (`audio-map/leads.ts`, pure): the picture lands before its music anchor, never after: cut and transition 1 frame (max 2), hit 1 (max 3), sfx 0. Shared by `edit:snap` and `src/motion/sync/beatGrid.ts`. `LIBRARY_TO_SHEET_KIND` maps block cue kinds to cue-sheet kinds.
- **Audio curves** (`audio-curves/`, `compute.ts` and `schema.ts` pure, `measure.ts` Node): `audio:curves` turns a map into per-frame 0..1 curves for audio-reactive motion. Without `--input` the levels resample `map.energyCurve` (no mid); with it ffmpeg decodes low (<150 Hz), mid and high (>2.5 kHz) , per-frame RMS normalised to the 99.5th percentile, attack/release followed. Envelopes peak at the hit's strength on `round(t*fps)`, max of overlaps. A grid with trust `none` gets no phases and no downbeat envelope. Stems come from `map.stems.dir`.
- **SFX synthesis** (`audio/sfx-synth.ts`, pure; `audio/sfx-pack.ts` writes the pack): seeded; `alignSec` marks each file's cue-frame sample. Author: `agentbros sfx-synth`.
- **Cue sounds** (`audio/cue-sounds.ts`, pure): `planCueSounds()` places sounds on block cues with the gain hierarchy and budget; `cueSoundTracks()` makes `audio:mix` tracks; `<CueSounds>` (`src/motion/sync`) plays them. With `library` (`librarySounds(index)`) it picks measured sounds by fit, seeded, synth as fallback. A hero cue's `layers` follow its primary (transient on the frame, body and tail +1, quieter, ≤ 0.2) within the budgets; a `decided` cue replaces undecided same-family sounds within 2 frames; `silent` places nothing and returns in `silences`.
- **Decisions** (`audio/cut-decisions.ts`, pure): every cut, transition and hero cue is a sound or a silence with a reason (`cueDecision`, `undecidedCues`; bare `sfx: "none"` is undecided). `decideFilmEvents()` matches plan boundaries (±1 frame) and block cuts (±2) to the cue sheet; `sfx:cues --motion-plan --cue-sheet` fails `VALIDATION` on the undecided, writes `decisions` and `silences`. `seamCues()`: `props.seamFrame` (block-relative perceived cut) moves a transition's cut cues; absent, the preview seam stays, with a warning.
- **Masking** (`audio/masking.ts`, pure on decoded samples; `audio/spectrum.ts` holds the FFT and 24 third-octave bands, 40 Hz–11 kHz at 22.05 kHz; `audio/masking-files.ts` decodes): `assessMasking()` reads each placed SFX in ±100 ms of its cue, per 11.6 ms frame, against music plus voice spread across bands (10 dB/band up, 20 down, −4 dB index; music ducked −9 dB where speech plays, as audio:mix does). Margin is the best band within 15 dB of the SFX's own peak; masked < 0 dB, marginal < 3. Collisions: two SFX sharing dominant bands within 200 ms (`layered` on one frame), or a bright SFX on a voice consonant (≥ 3 kHz). Fixes are `gain` (to the weight's ceiling, ≤ 6 dB) and `duck` (≤ 6 dB) as `safe`; `shift`, `filter`, `swap` are advice. `duckKeyframes()` turns ducks into absolute audio:mix keyframes (50 ms down, 150 ms up, merged, multiplied into existing keyframes), tagged `source: "fix-masking"` with the scaled keyframe's `baseVolume`; `stripFixMasking()` undoes them (the mixer ignores both fields). `maskingFromFiles()` builds music and voice on the film timeline from a mix plan's tracks (`startSec`, `sourceStartSec`, `volume`, keyframes via `volumeAt()`, loop; rate read as 1) and uses its `duckDb`, else the flags, else music at 1 with no voice; `report.inputs` records which (the plan is the source: `mix-report.json` has no tracks). A model, not hearing.
- **Sync map** (`audio/syncmap.ts`, pure; `audio/syncmap-files.ts` draws from a render): `buildSyncMap()` gives each cut its frame error to the nearest beat (negative leads); red over 1 frame, and a structural cut (within 0.25 s of a phrase or section start, or snapped to a downbeat or phrase) must sit within 1 frame of a downbeat, unless `grid.barKnown` is false (beats only; missing counts as known). Each cut has a `verdict` (`miss` is red). With a `reliable` grid it follows these rules; on `weak` or `none` (`verdictWeight: "low"`, `gridNote`) only a cut within 0.15 s of a phrase, section or key-moment start is judged, against that `anchor`; beat offsets are grey. Cuts: declared cuts (`declaredCuts()`: kinds cut, transition, ground-swap) merged with detected ones within 2 frames (`mergeCuts()`), `source` declared/detected/both; `classifySpike()` calls a detected change a cut when its luma-difference peak is ≥ 2x the larger neighbour, else a move (no verdict); a declared cut is measured on its own spike within 2 frames when there is one. Cuts outside the grid's beat span are judged as on a weak grid. The critic pack's `offCuts` holds only misses; on a weak grid `beatOffsetsAdvisory` lists the off-beat cuts apart. `drawSyncMap()` draws waveform, energy, grid, cuts to their beats, cues and SFX (orange when masked); the critic pack writes `syncmap.png`/`.json` and `sound.json`'s `syncmap`.
- **SFX index and CLAP** (`audio/sfx-index.ts`, `audio/sfx-clap.ts`): `buildSfxMeasureIndex()` measures files through `scripts/workers/sfx-index.py` (locked uv `sfx-index` group) into `sfx-index.json`; `constraintFit()` scores a measurement against wanted length, peak, tail and dynamics. `runClap()` runs `scripts/workers/sfx-clap.py` (locked uv `clap` group, `laion/larger_clap_general`, weights in the Hugging Face cache) for embeddings (`sfx-clap.json`, base64 float32 by sha256) and tag scores. The collection lives in the git-ignored `public/shared/sfx/<kind>/`; only each kind's `manifest.json` (source, licence, attribution, recipe, processing, sha256), `sfx-index.json` and `sfx-clap.json` are committed. `audio/sfx-fetch.ts`: `fetchSfx({kind?, id?})` rebuilds the audio and returns a status per sound (never throws per sound); `freezeProjectSfx()` copies used sounds into a project with provenance.
- **Earshot in the flow** ([guide](../../../docs/earshot-flow.md)): `audio/sfx-audition.ts` ranks hero-cue candidates (`AUDITION_CRITERIA`; `PlannedCue.sound` places the choice); `audio/sfx-selection-check.ts` is film:done's `hero-sounds-selected`; `audio/review-evidence.ts` reads saved audits by bytes; `audio/sfx-map.ts` maps a reference's sound events. Advisory; `earshot.py` stays unchanged (hash pinned).
