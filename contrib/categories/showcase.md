# Showcase entries

## What is valuable

Contribute a real harness-made film with provenance. Inspect `src/motion/showcase/`, `public/motion-lab/showcase/PROVENANCE.md` and `pnpm library:usage --help` for the current showcase implementation.

## Not wanted

Invented examples, unverifiable cost or subscription claims, project footprints, restricted sources or media binaries in git.

## Definition of done

Preserve source rights and render identity; declare actual harness, model, commit and costs without inferring subscription billing. Gallery `showcases/<slug>/showcase.json` submissions require registry support before a PR; use the showcase issue form to propose them.

## Evidence required

Real film link, provenance and rights declaration; include delivery receipt/snapshot and verification level where available. Keep preview media outside git.

## Gates

- `pnpm typecheck`
