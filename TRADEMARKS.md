Pictures** and **agentbros** (in plain text, not the logos or mascot artwork, except where stated) when the use is truthful, does not suggest that the Licensor sponsors or endorses you or your work, and does not confuse anyone about the source of a product or service:

- **Referring to the project.** Say that you use, study, teach, write about, review, compare or contribute to Agent Bros. Pictures, and link to the official repository.

The licence allows you to modify and redistribute the software. If you publish or distribute a modified version, or host one for anyone, you must:

- give it a different name that is not confusingly similar to the marks;

You may say, truthfully, that your version is "based on" or "a fork of" Agent Bros. Pictures, in plain text and less prominently than your own name.
