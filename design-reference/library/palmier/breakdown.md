**Important: I cannot hear the sound.** The conclusions about sound are drawn from `audio.json`, the waveform and `sync.json`. There are no recognised words even after a second run with English set as the language, so I cannot say whether the film has a voice.

**Idea.** A person gives a real tool three simple requests, and the beginning of the film itself turns out to be the first result.

**Hook (0–3 s).** A short two-part statement. A passing person takes the first part off the screen, then measured silence at 1.17–1.75 s, then the second part with an ellipsis.

**Structure.** The way to the product through a real desk (3–7 s). Three requests in rising order of difficulty, preceded by doubt on black. People's reactions of 0.6–0.7 s each. A final burst of shots (33.88–36.64 s). The name on black.

**Editing.** The detector found 33 cuts; after review there are 59 (60 shots), 34 of them in the last 2.76 s. Before the burst the pace is calm: 25 cuts in 33.88 s, median shot 1.12 s. A supposed cut at 25.80 s turned out, on frame-by-frame review, to be a smooth camera pull-back on the same recording; at 11.12 s, conversely, there is a hard cut onto an already blurred log scroll.

I chose the window after already seeing the peaks, so this is a hint, not proof.

**Moves.**
1. **Burst of 2-frame shots (33.88–36.64 s).** The eyes are aligned to the centre and the caption is still: no single frame can be read, yet a sense of abundance remains and one sentence reads.
2. **The camera pushes into the monitor (4.52–5.20 s), reverse pull-back (23.12–24.04 s).** The camera passes a blurred bottle (0.24 s) and brakes on the dock (0.44 s); the pull-back accelerates for 1 s and breaks off with a 3-frame fade. The interface gets a real place, so it is believed.
3. **The result grows out of the preview (32.88–33.88 s).** The preview grows evenly for 0.48 s until the interface is gone, then the face slowly pushes in for another 12 frames; there is no cut.
4. **Recognition (21.40–23.16 s).** The tool's output turns out to be the beginning of this film, so nothing needs to be claimed.
5. **Black screen and silence (36.64–37.56 s).** The sound cuts out 0.39 s after the cut, and in the silence a single change appears.

**What we can repeat.** The real interface via `ScreenPlayback` and `RegionCamera`, the preview growth, the doubt cards, the ending. A working demo is needed: without it none of this will happen.

**What is impossible or expensive.** A live camera at the monitor and people's reactions: a shoot is needed. The move with the film's own beginning suits only a video tool; for another product there is only a related variant. The burst needs many images, and the project budget is currently 0.

**What we do not take.** Their brand, footage, images, copy and music. Only the method.
