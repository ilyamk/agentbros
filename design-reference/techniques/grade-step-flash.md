name: Grade steps on a held plate as the cut
intent: The cut becomes an event in the picture itself, without a wipe: the plate stays framed while its whole grade jumps.
route: remotion

**What it does.** Swaps the whole frame's grade for one to three frames at a time on a plate that does not move: monochrome, photographic negative, a duotone in two tokens (with an RGB fringe), a polarity flip, a halftone, a ground blink (flat ground, the subject in light dots), a white bleach that decays over its step, a horizontal sine wave in bands, the subject darkened on black, a supplied subject matte tinted on dark (the x-ray), or any ground and ink colour with or without the plate (a content-free colour flash; an inverted title). The next plate enters on one negative frame. Optionally the incoming plate arrives in seeded horizontal slices pushed sideways that resolve in steps, its first frame polarity-flipped. Block: `grade-step-flash`.

**When it fits.** A cut between macro or object plates in a cool, technical film; a subject swap that should feel like a signal; the hook's first frames. Once per second at most.

**When it does not.** Warm, calm films; every cut of a film (it stops being an event); interface footage that must stay readable.

**Timing.** Measured in airform F71-F79: mono 3, negative 2, cream duotone with fringe 2, cut, incoming negative 1, normal from the next frame. Abdulah's duotone flashes are 1-2 frames before the cut, gojiberry's flash frames 2-4, nowakdesign's ground blinks 3 frames (1-2 at the end). paper-style swaps the picture under a bleach: near white 1 frame (F717, diff 37.6), back over 2 (`PAPER_BLEACH_ENTER`). abdulah t10 leaves through dot screen 1, plate 1, dot screen 2, sine wave with RGB split 2, then the cut (F634-F640, `ABDULAH_WAVE_EXIT`). gojiberry darkens the subject on black for 4 frames (F492-F495, `GOJIBERRY_DARK_EXIT`) or shows a cyan skeleton matte for 4 (F644-F647, `gojiberryXrayExit(src)`). nowakdesign opens on navy, black and white for 3 frames each, black 2, the title from F11, the frame inverted to white with faint tinted type F16-F22, back at F23 with an RGB split F24-F25 (`NOWAK_STROBE_ENTER`); our render matches it frame for frame (`motion:strip --vs` against 0-0.9 s). Our render matches airform frame for frame (out/qa strip against 2.30-2.70 s).

**Read.** `src/motion/library/effects/GradeStepFlash.tsx`, schedules in `src/motion/library/effects/flash-plan.ts` (`gradeSchedule`, `gradeChanges`, `sliceOffsets`, `bleachLevel`, `waveOffsets` and the presets).

**Pitfalls.** A dark plate inverted is near-white: each step is a potential flash. The default event is five picture changes, within WCAG 2.3.1's six transitions a second; a second event inside the same second, or `chaos` 1 on the slice (bands re-rolled each step), can fail motion:check `FLASH_RISK`, so render and check. The grade steps are safe by count (tests/motion-blocks-effects-refs.test.ts); the slice's band steps are not countable that way, and their safety rests on the rendered check (the default preview passes). The slice and the wave render the plate once per band: keep sound outside. The x-ray needs a real matte of the subject: `src`, artwork drawn in place of the plate (`gojiberryXrayExit`), or `matteSrc`, the plate subject's alpha cut-out (a PNG the plate's size, transparent around the subject), which masks the plate into a tinted negative of the subject (`gojiberryXrayFromCutout`); that reads close to gojiberry's x-ray but is not a skeleton. Without either the `matte` step renders `darken`, and no step fakes a skeleton. The repository has no local, free cut-out route (no rembg, no segmentation worker; Apple Vision's foreground-instance mask would be the candidate for a new `scripts/workers` Swift worker): supply the PNG or make it with `pnpm rmbg` (fal birefnet, paid, after the user's approval). The nowakdesign strobe has eight cuts in its first second, over the six-change count, but the navy to black step is under 10% luminance, so the flash sensor sees two flashes; its presets and the bleach, wave and dark presets pass a frame-by-frame modelled sensor on black and white plates (tests/motion-blocks-gap-effects.test.ts) and `flash:check` on their renders. Four bleaches in a second fail it.
