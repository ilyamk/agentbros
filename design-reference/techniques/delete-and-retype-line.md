**What it does.** A first line types and holds. Then the whole line is removed on screen: backspaced by word chunk or by character, shown selected and deleted in one key, or cut out on one frame. The caret (if any) waits alone a few frames, and the second line types in its place. The viewer reads the first line as a claim or a placeholder and the second as what replaces it. Library block `type-caret-track` with the `retypeLine` prop (`lines`, `deleteMode`, `deleteRate`, `deleteStep`, `markFrames`, `hold`, `emptyFrames`, `retypeUnit`, `retypeFramesPerUnit`); presets `caretTrackRetypeWordPreset` (abdulah's timing) and `caretTrackRetypeCharPreset` (cashfree's).

**When it fits.** A thesis the film contradicts (type the common belief, delete it, type the counter-claim); a placeholder replaced by the real line, to show work being made; a hook where the act of rewriting is the point.

**When it does not.** The first line must be read before it goes: under about 15 frames of hold it reads as a glitch. Not for a single word swapped inside a held line (that is `selection-retype-slot`, `type-word-rotate` with `swap: "retype"`). Not for a product's real editor: record the real one (`capture:app`).

**Timing.** Both films are 30 fps, so no conversion; abdulah animates on twos (each picture held 2 frames), so its rates are per 2-frame step. Measured frame by frame from local strips (`visuals/a1/retype-typing.jpg`, `retype-delete.jpg` in abdulah; `retype-caret.jpg`, `s017-backspace.jpg`, `retype-keys.jpg` in cashfree).

- abdulah 0.13-4.33 s (`t01-typewriter-backspace-hook`): an eight-word serif line, no caret in any frame. Typed by character in bursts that follow the voice, F4-F96 (46 characters in 92 frames, 2.0 a character on average, about 0.7 inside a burst); a new glyph appears grey for one step, then white. Held 16 frames (F96-F112). The last word turns grey for one step (F112-F113), then the line goes backwards in five word chunks of one or two words, one every 2 frames (F114, F116, F118, F120, F122: 7 words in 10 frames, about 0.7 words a frame); one more word greys for a step on the way (F118-F119). The first word stays: held 4 frames (F122-F125), slides right accelerating over 4 frames (F126-F129) and is hard-swapped to its replacement on F130 (no scramble: the diff tiles that looked like glyph noise are the moving word over itself). This occurrence differs in the second version: single words replace each other at advancing positions (F130, F144, F148) rather than typing as one line. The line is never empty: the kept word fills the 8 frames between the last chunk and the swap. Sound: `audio.json` onsets at 3.808, 3.878, 4.017 and 4.087 s (F114.2, F116.3, F120.5, F122.6) sit on four of the five deletion steps (none on F118); one more at 4.156 s (F124.7) falls in the kept word's hold before its slide. Under the narration, so what they are is inferred.
- cashfree 18.63-21.73 s (`canvas-type-becomes-live-page`): a three-word line in a grotesque inside a thin selection box on a white canvas, bar caret. Typed by word, not character: F564, F570, F574 (5 frames a word). Caret solid while keys arrive and 12 frames after the last (F574-F585); on F586 it leaves the line end as a drag selection of one word starts, so no idle blink is seen. Held 40 frames (F574-F614), during which that word is selected and restyled (F586-F611). Backspaced by character from F614 to F622: 19 characters in 9 frames, 2, 0, 2, 4, 3, 3, 2, 0, 3 a frame (about 2.1 a frame, 0.47 frames a character); the centred line re-centres as it shrinks. Empty box F622-F627 (6 frames, caret back on F623). The headline retypes by word: F628, F631, F635, F637, F642, F644, F648, F652 (8 words in 24 frames, 3.4 frames a word, about 0.45 frames a character), wrapping to a second line at F642, where the frame starts its punch-in. Sound: `audio.json` onsets lie on a regular grid of about 0.19 s from the music; none falls on an individual delete key.

Ours: `caretTrackRetypeWordPreset` holds 16, greys the last word 2 frames, deletes words on twos at 0.67 a frame (the whole line), then 6 empty frames in place of abdulah's 8-frame hold, slide and swap of the kept word; `caretTrackRetypeCharPreset` types by word at 5 frames, holds 40, deletes 2.1 characters a frame, 6 empty frames, retypes by word at 3.4 frames, caret solid 12 frames after a key (the 8-frame off phase is ours: cashfree never shows one). Compared side by side with both spans (`motion:strip --vs`).

**Read.** `src/motion/type-track.ts` (`retypeLinePlan`, `deleteSteps`), `src/motion/library/typography/CaretTrack.tsx`, `tests/motion-blocks-gap-typography.test.ts`.

**Pitfalls.** Old notes on both films read averages as rates: cashfree's "1 and 2 characters a frame" for the typing are word keys averaged over characters, and abdulah's deletion is 0.7 words a frame on twos, not 2 frames a word. A doubled frame on twos is not a hold. The hold is counted from the first line's last key, not from the block's start, so the first line's length does not shift the delete. With `deleteMode: "select-all"` or `"cut"` the line goes on one frame; `markFrames` is then the selection shown before it.
